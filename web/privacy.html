Privacy Policy

App: Refill — Board Game Scores • Last updated: 2026-09-10

This Privacy Policy explains how Refill (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services (the “App”). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: the e-mail address and username you provide when you create an account, or the name and e-mail address shared by Google or Apple if you sign in with those services.
  • Content you create: photos of score sheets you add, the names you give them, game names and dates, and your handwriting on a sheet. Handwriting is stored as coordinate data (pen strokes), not as an image of your hand.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Individual or Family plan and its renewal date, as reported by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Family plan membership: which family group you belong to and the invite links you create or use.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not collect precise location. Your language preference is stored only on your device.

How We Use Information

  • Provide the core features: keeping your sheets, games, and handwriting and syncing them across your devices.
  • Apply plan limits (for example the number of photo sheets on the free plan) and family plan seats.
  • Secure accounts and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

Your Photos Stay Private

  • Score sheet photos are stored in a private storage area that only your account can access. They are never made public, never listed for other users, and never used as templates for others.
  • We do not analyze your photos with machine learning and we do not use your content to train any model.
  • The Family plan shares subscription benefits, not sheets. Family members cannot see each other's sheets, games, or handwriting.
  • Deleting a sheet in the App deletes its photo from our servers.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of providers to run the App. They process data only as needed to deliver their service and under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (servers located in Singapore).
  • Google and Apple — optional sign-in.
  • App Store and Google Play — subscription billing. RevenueCat — subscription status management.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We share data with the service providers above under contracts that require confidentiality and data protection.
  • We may disclose information to comply with law, protect rights, or respond to lawful requests.

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Deleting a sheet removes that sheet, its photo, and its games. Deleting your account removes all of your data as described on the Account Deletion page.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Access / update: you can view and edit your sheets, games, and profile in the App.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and all associated data from within the App (Profile → Delete account) or by contacting us.
  • Subscriptions: you manage and cancel subscriptions through your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Marketing: we do not send marketing communications.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection) under laws such as the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will remove it.

International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Singapore where our servers are located. We take steps to protect your data in accordance with this Policy.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, photos are stored in private buckets, and database access is restricted per user with row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
