How to Delete Your Refill Account

You can permanently delete your Refill account and all associated data directly from within the app.

  1. Open the Refill app.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Once confirmed, the following is permanently removed from our servers: your account, all sheets and their photos, all games and handwriting, and any family plan membership. This cannot be undone.

An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings to avoid further charges.

If you cannot access the app, e-mail us from the address of your account at REFILL_SUPPORT_EMAIL and we will delete it within 30 days.
